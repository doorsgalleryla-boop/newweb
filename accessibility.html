Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Doors Gallery is committed to making doorsgallery.net accessible and usable for people with disabilities. We are working to improve the experience for all visitors and to apply recognized accessibility practices.

1. Accessibility Goal and Status

Our goal is to support the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. The website has not yet undergone a complete independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim that every page or feature fully conforms. We continue to review and improve the website as issues are identified.

2. Measures We Take

Our accessibility work includes:

  • Providing descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Using headings, labels, landmarks, and other semantic HTML to support assistive technologies.
  • Maintaining readable text and sufficient color contrast.
  • Supporting keyboard use for navigation and interactive controls.
  • Designing responsive pages that can be used at different screen sizes and zoom levels.
  • Reviewing new content and features with accessibility in mind.

3. Known Limitations

Some content may not yet work perfectly with every assistive technology, browser, or device. Product photographs and older content may also vary in the level of descriptive detail provided. If you encounter a barrier, please tell us which page or feature caused the problem so we can investigate it.

4. Feedback and Assistance

We welcome feedback about the accessibility of our website. If you have difficulty accessing content, using a feature, or requesting an estimate, contact us:

  • Email: doorsgalleryla@gmail.com
  • Phone: (747) 212-7222

Please identify the page, feature, or information you need and describe the issue if possible. We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within five business days and will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service through an accessible alternative.

5. Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We will continue reviewing the website, addressing reported barriers, and updating this statement as our accessibility work develops.

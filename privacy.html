Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Doors Gallery (“Doors Gallery,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and retain personal information when you visit doorsgallery.net, contact us, request an estimate, visit our showroom, or purchase products or services from us.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, phone number, email address, billing address, or delivery address.
  • Project and transaction information, such as door models, measurements, quantities, finishes, project address, estimates, invoices, payment status, delivery details, and installation preferences.
  • Communications, including messages, emails, calls, feedback, and requests you send to us.
  • Website and device information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, and approximate location that may be recorded in standard hosting or security logs.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the website’s general contact or estimate fields.

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to questions and prepare estimates.
  • Process orders, payments, deliveries, returns, and installations.
  • Provide customer service and communicate about a project or transaction.
  • Maintain business, tax, warranty, safety, and accounting records.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, customers, personnel, and property.

3. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Service providers that support hosting, email, payment processing, accounting, delivery, installation, security, and other business operations.
  • Contractors or project partners involved in fulfilling your requested order or service.
  • Government authorities, courts, insurers, professional advisers, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and safety.
  • A buyer, successor, or adviser in connection with a merger, sale, financing, or transfer of all or part of the business.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

As of the date above, our website does not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. Our website host and security providers may use essential technologies or maintain server logs needed to deliver and protect the website. If our practices materially change, we will update this policy and provide any choices required by law.

5. Retention and Security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including customer service, warranties, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

You may also have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising a privacy right.
